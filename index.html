FAQ

Good questions.

Can’t find what you need? Contact support and we’ll help.

Is HomedIn really free?
Yes. Your personal plan, reminders, task guides and Homebook are free for one home, and you get starter credits to try HomedIn AI. HomedIn+ is an optional upgrade.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There’s no sign-up and no login. Your home data stays on your device and syncs between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud.
How does HomedIn know what my home needs?
During setup you tell it your ZIP code, when the home was built, the roof, foundation, exterior and systems. HomedIn uses real local frost dates and climate risks such as wildfire, hurricane and ice-dam seasons to time each task, and only shows tasks that apply to your home.
What is Ask AI, and is it safe to use?
Ask AI is an optional assistant powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. It asks your permission before it’s used for the first time, and you choose which records it can use. HomedIn sends only what’s needed to answer, such as your home’s facts, your question, any photo you choose and your approximate area. It never sends your name, street address or exact location to the AI. If you use optional address auto-fill, your address goes to our server and RentCast to look up public county records, and it isn’t stored. HomedIn’s server doesn’t keep your questions, photos or home facts, and Anthropic doesn’t train on them and deletes them within 30 days. Ask AI is available in the US at launch. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
Are AI cost estimates real quotes?
No. They’re estimated ranges to help you plan and compare. Real prices vary by region, home and contractor. Always get written quotes, and use a licensed professional for gas, electrical panel, structural and roof work. HomedIn AI always tells you when a job needs a pro.
What happens to my data and privacy?
HomedIn has no ads and no tracking, and nothing is linked to you. Your data lives on your devices and in your private iCloud, which we can’t see. The only analytics we collect are anonymous thumbs-down and concern counts, with no content. The full details, including the App Store privacy label, are in our Privacy Policy.
I already bought HomedIn Pro. What changes?
Nothing you paid for goes away. Lifetime owners keep every Pro feature forever, and monthly members keep their current price. You’ll also get a founding-member offer if you’d like to add HomedIn+.
Which devices does it work on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26.4 or later.
I’m a real estate agent. Can I give HomedIn to clients?
Yes. HomedIn makes a thoughtful closing gift. See HomedIn for realtors to learn more and join the agent early-access list.
