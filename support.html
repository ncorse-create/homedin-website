Support

How can we help?

Send us a note and a real person will get back to you, usually within two business days.

Or email us directly at persueapps@gmail.com

Restore a purchase

In HomedIn, go to More > Subscription > Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Sync between iPhone and iPad

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices and make sure iCloud is turned on for HomedIn in Settings > your name > iCloud.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Reminders not showing up?

Check Settings > Notifications > HomedIn, and quiet hours in HomedIn’s own reminder settings.

Your data and privacy

No account, no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy, or see the FAQ.
